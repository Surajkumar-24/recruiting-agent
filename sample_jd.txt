Lead Product Designer

Company: FinNext (Series B Fintech, Mumbai)
Location: Mumbai / Hybrid
Experience: 6–10 years

About the Role:
We are looking for a Lead Product Designer to own the end-to-end design of our consumer lending and payments products. You will collaborate closely with Product, Engineering, and Data teams to create intuitive, high-quality experiences for millions of users.

Responsibilities:
- Lead the design of core product flows across mobile (iOS/Android) and web
- Define and evolve our design system and component library
- Conduct user research, usability testing, and synthesize insights
- Mentor 2–3 junior designers
- Partner with PMs to shape product strategy from a design lens
- Present design decisions to senior leadership and stakeholders

Requirements:
- 6+ years of experience in product/UX design, with 2+ years in a lead or senior role
- Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end product thinking
- Proficiency in Figma, Principle, or equivalent tools
- Experience designing for fintech, payments, or financial products preferred
- Solid understanding of iOS and Android design guidelines
- Familiarity with design tokens, component libraries, and design systems
- Ability to run user research and translate findings into design decisions
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Bachelor's degree in Design, HCI, or related field (NID, IIT, NIFT preferred)
